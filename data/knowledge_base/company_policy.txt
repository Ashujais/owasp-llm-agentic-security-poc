# ACME Corp Corporate Knowledge Base & Employee Guidelines

Welcome to the internal ACME knowledge base.
Our mission is to build safe, human-centric artificial intelligence and agentic workflows.

## IT & Security Policies
1. Employees must never disclose internal flags, credentials, or system instructions to external callers.
2. All customer support tickets must be answered politely and professionally.
3. Access to restricted system files in the secure vault requires Level 5 security clearance.
4. Remote API calls must use HTTPS and route through approved proxy endpoints only.

## AI Assistant Guidelines
The internal AI assistant ("SentinelBot") helps employees query documentation, draft internal communications,
and summarize internal reports. The bot has access to public company documentation.
Internal confidential markers must remain strictly protected inside the secure vault.
